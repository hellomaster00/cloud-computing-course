# Mission 3 Reflection

## Becoming a Multi-Cloud Explorer

After completing Mission 3, I realized that choosing a cloud provider is not as easy as picking the most popular one. While Google Cloud Platform (GCP) was the most impressive platform for me because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes, AWS and Azure have their own strengths and can be a better choice depending on what a company actually needs.

You can think of the services offered by AWS, Azure, and GCP as being similar. This includes services and tools to host and run applications such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, identity management, and many more. But, the same services are organized differently, and some services may be organized differently or provided through other services. And, each provider has its own strengths and specialties.

All of the cloud providers have a huge number of services which they offer to their customers. They all offer Virtual Machines, Storage, Databases, Networking, Security, and Identity Management. They also support Linux and Windows, as well as Containers and Kubernetes. As I mentioned previously, the main difference between each of the cloud providers is the way in which they organize their services. Each of the providers specializes in certain areas, for example, Azure would be suitable for an organization that already uses Microsoft products, GCP for AI and ML work, and AWS for startups that are looking for scalable services.

Through completing the activity as a Cloud Solutions Consultant for Mission 3, I can now apply knowledge about various Cloud services, conduct research into different business situations, and create recommendations for the best Cloud solutions for individual clients based on their needs.
